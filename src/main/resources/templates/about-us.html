CampusHive was founded and engineered by Anuj Chourasiya, a backend-focused software developer passionate about scalable systems, secure architectures, and impactful student solutions. The platform was built to solve a common campus challenge: students often miss valuable events, workshops, hackathons, and networking opportunities. CampusHive centralizes everything into one intelligent ecosystem, making campus life more connected and accessible.
