OUR MISSION & VISION

Building the Future of Campus Engagement

CampusHive is designed to bring students, student organizers, hosts, HODs, and institutions together under one seamless event management ecosystem. We simplify event discovery, custom questionnaire registrations, check-ins, ticketing, and university community building.

Anuj Chourasiya

3rd Year Software Developer & Founder

CampusHive was conceived, designed, and engineered by Anuj Chourasiya. As a backend-focused developer, Anuj is passionate about building secure architectures, high-performance APIs, database schemas, and intuitive web solutions.

The platform was inspired by a universal campus problem: students missing out on key workshops, hackathons, seminars, and networking sessions due to fragmented communication. CampusHive aggregates all public and campus-only opportunities into a single interface.

Why CampusHive?

explore Centralized Discoverability

One unified feed showing all local public events and campus-restricted registrations. Students never have to browse dozens of Slack/WhatsApp groups again.

assignment Custom Registration Forms

Hosts can construct custom forms on the fly with custom inputs, drop-downs, and files, which are securely verified and stored in database schemas.

qr_code_scanner Easy QR Code Check-ins

Each registration generates an entry pass QR code. HODs and hosts can scan codes directly from their browsers to mark attendance instantly.

verified_user Multi-role Access Control

Integrated role management ensures HODs, hosts, and students have secure and customized access permissions across departments and campuses.
