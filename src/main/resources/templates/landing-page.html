Questions

Frequently asked

How do I create an event?
Go to your dashboard and select "Create event." The setup wizard walks you through venue selection, ticket tiers, and promotional assets in a few minutes — no manual configuration required.
Can we track ticket sales in real time?
Yes. The live dashboard shows conversion rate, remaining capacity, and discount code usage as sales happen, with exportable financial reports for your department.
Is support available for student attendees?
Support is available around the clock for both administrators and attendees — from a lost ticket to a setup question, the campus success team responds directly.
